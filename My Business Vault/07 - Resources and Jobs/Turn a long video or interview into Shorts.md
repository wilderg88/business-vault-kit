# Turn a long video or interview into Shorts

**The job:** take a long video (an interview, a market walkthrough, a live) and turn it into short vertical clips with a hook and caption each, for the owner to approve before anything is posted.

## Boot chain (read these, in order, and you have the skill)
1. This note, end to end.
2. The brand voice note in `05 - Marketing and Content`, if there is one: how the owner sounds.
3. [[Settled-Facts]]: so dates, prices and places in captions are right.
4. Only the event note the video is about, if any.

## The procedure
1. Get the video's transcript (from the platform's captions, or ask the owner for it). Read all of it.
2. Pick 3 to 5 moments of 15 to 45 seconds that stand on their own: a strong opinion, a story, a tip, a funny or warm moment. Note start and end times.
3. For each clip write: a hook for the first 2 seconds (the words on screen and spoken), a caption of 1 to 3 short lines, and a call to action (for example "See you Saturday at ...", using real details from Settled-Facts).
4. Put the list in a draft note in `05 - Marketing and Content` named "Shorts from [video name] [date]" and update that folder's index.
5. Show the owner. Only the clips and captions the owner approves get made or posted, and only by the owner or with the owner's yes.

## Quality bar
- Every clip makes sense with no context from the rest of the video.
- Hooks are specific, not generic ("Our first market had 3 vendors" beats "You won't believe this").
- No dates, prices or places that aren't in Settled-Facts or the event note.
- No music or footage the owner doesn't have the right to use. Anyone else in the clip has agreed to be in it.
- Nothing is posted without the owner's yes.

## Lessons (fold corrections in here over time)
- (Example, made up: "Keep captions under 12 words; the owner's audience reads on phones.")
