# Customers and FAQ

Your customers and the questions they ask. The FAQ note holds each common question with the answer you actually give. When a customer asks something new and you answer it, the assistant adds it to the FAQ in the same turn, once you approve it. Official answers on hours, prices and policies live in [[Settled-Facts]]; the FAQ points there instead of repeating them.

**Keeping this list current:** whenever a note in this folder is created, renamed, moved or changed in a meaningful way, the assistant updates the list below (one line per note) in the same pass. When a note moves to another folder, it comes off this list and goes onto that folder's list.

## Notes in this folder
- [[FAQ]]: common customer and vendor questions with your approved answers
