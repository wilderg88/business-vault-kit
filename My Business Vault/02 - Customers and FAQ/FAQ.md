# FAQ

Common questions from customers and vendors, each with the answer I approved. The assistant drafts replies from these answers, and adds new ones the same day I answer something new. Hours, prices and policies are in [[Settled-Facts]]; answers here link to them instead of repeating them, so there is only one place to change.

**Format for each question:**

### [Question, in the words people usually use]
- **Who asks:** customer / vendor
- **How often:** [rough count or "often"]
- **Answer:** [the answer I approved, in my voice]
- **Approved:** [date]

(Example with made-up data, delete once real questions are added.)

- **Who asks:** customer
- **How often:** often
